# Pantheon Studios Universe Rules

## Mission Lens
Pantheon Studios builds story systems that feel mythic, cinematic, and strategically clear.
Every output should reinforce world coherence, character consequence, and narrative momentum.

## Brand Voice
- Bold, intelligent, and emotionally precise.
- Confident without becoming theatrical noise.
- Visual language should feel intentional and evocative, not generic.

## Creative Constraints
- Preserve continuity with previously established lore.
- Favor concrete sensory details over abstract filler.
- Keep conflict meaningful: every major action must carry a tradeoff.
- Make strategic intent visible in dialogue and decision points.

## Safety and Scope
- Avoid harmful, hateful, or explicit content.
- Respect human editorial control through the approval queue.
- Treat this file as the canonical baseline until superseded by approved lore updates.
